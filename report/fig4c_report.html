What was rebuilt, and the judgement calls

  • Data. Public parquet release; the pre-registered preprocessing from the repo's live_loading.py was ported and yields the paper's 1047 rounds exactly.
  • Statements. Initial winner = the statement participants critiqued; revised winner = the statement shown in the end-of-round survey; “statements” = all model candidates shown at that iteration.
  • Embedding. The SM names Sentence-T5 (768-d) without a size; the public sentence-transformers/sentence-t5-* ports were used with a 512-token limit.
  • Minority. Side of neutral with fewer pre-deliberation ratings; neutral raters are non-minority (SM 5.4.1). Tied rounds and unanimous rounds have no minority.
  • Regression. Weights ≥ 0 summing to 1, fitted by constrained least squares; SEs are the OLS standard errors of the active-set fit, as the paper reports, plus a cluster bootstrap over rounds.
